### ENGINEERING PROFILE

I’m a Computer Engineering student at Kettering University with hands-on experience connecting **hardware, sensors, PCBs, and embedded control**. My projects range from a dual-mode autonomous/manual F1-style RC car to water-quality monitoring, custom PCB design, and analog audio electronics.

I contributed to the RC car and water-filtration systems as part of a **Schoolcraft Engineering Club collaboration**. My individual work includes a custom Arduino shield PCB, an IC-based line follower, and an LM386 audio amplifier. I’m interested in automotive electronics, embedded systems, firmware, and hardware testing.

### SELECTED PROJECTS

| Project | What I built or contributed |
|---|---|
| [Dual-Mode Autonomous/Manual F1 RC Car](https://github.com/Zarraf16/Autonomous-Car) · **Engineering Club collaboration** | Contributed to PCB design and hardware integration. The vehicle used six IR sensors with PID line-following control and Pixy2 traffic-light detection. Video timing over 2.65 m estimated 19.8 mph for the 2015 model and 22.2 mph for the 2023 model; results include a 10% uncertainty allowance. |
| [Automated Water Filtration System](https://github.com/Zarraf16/Water-Filter-Sensor-Module) · **Engineering Club collaboration** | Integrated Arduino control, water-quality sensing, display, and automatic pump operation. Team testing addressed filter selection, pump and converter choices, pipe sizing/material, fittings, and system-code integration. |
| [Custom Arduino Shield PCB](https://github.com/Zarraf16/Arduino-LED-Shield-PCB) · **Individual** | Designed a two-layer FR-4 board in EasyEDA, from schematic and footprints through routing and Gerber generation. The 44.58 × 23.62 mm layout used 35 mil power traces and GND stitching vias. |
| [LM386 Audio Amplifier](https://github.com/Zarraf16/Lm386-Audio-amplifier) · **Individual** | Built a 9 V mono amplifier for a 4 Ω, 10 W speaker; reviewed the circuit in LTspice and measured response from 100 Hz to 15 kHz. |
| [IC-Based Line Follower](https://github.com/Zarraf16/IC-Based-Line-Follower) · **Individual** | Built and tested an IR-sensor line follower using logic ICs and PCB-mounted components. |
| [Automatic Plant Watering System](https://github.com/Zarraf16/Automatic-Plant-Watering-System) · **Prototype** | Developing an Arduino soil-moisture monitor with relay-controlled pump operation. |
